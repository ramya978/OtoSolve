03

Publication & notice

Public description and donor-facing terms.

Check every detail above before you launch — launching schedules this campaign.

Why this record exists

A campaign ties every donation to one fund or programme, with a target, a time window and the wording donors see. Receipts and reports follow the campaign code.

Protected core

The campaign ID stays permanent and the code is locked after launch. Dates, amounts and wording can change later with a recorded reason.

Where it connects

  • Funds & programmes
  • Donors & leads
  • Payments & receipts
  • Employees
